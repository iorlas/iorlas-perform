# shorts: originality and demonetisation guardrails

Policy: YouTube's "inauthentic content" (renamed from "repetitious" on 2025-07-15), fetched 2026-11-05 from https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392. Full quotes and the gate are in `${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/../world-bootstrap/distinctness.md`; this file is what a Shorts author must keep in mind.

- **Violating:** "looks like it's made with a template"; "similar or repetitive content with low educational value, commentary, narratives, or minimal variation across videos"; "templated storylines... characters put in the same situation over and over again with the same outcome"; AI content "made with generic or unoriginal templates giving the impression of mass production".
- **Allowed:** each video "materially varied"; a series following a set of characters across episodes if each has "a distinct storyline, focus, or concept"; recurring intros/outros if the bulk differs; AI used to visualise "a unique character and narrative you invented"; templates and tools are fine if "the final product must still demonstrate your creative vision".
- **Risk:** a violation can remove the **whole channel's** monetisation eligibility (3P); detection also looks at titles, thumbnails and descriptions (3P).
- **Not covered by that page (OPEN, unverified):** animation and synthetic voice as such. AI disclosure is a separate rule.

## Guard rules for every Short
1. Distinct premise, central joke mechanism, outcome, main set, title/cover pattern, structure versus the last 5 episodes; at most one dimension repeats.
2. Recurring cast, rigs, corner mark and sting are the series; the bulk of each video is new.
3. Titles, covers and descriptions are written per episode, not filled from one template string.
4. Original script and voices; a human rewrite and review at each stop; keep the evidence (design sheets, table-read objections, ledger).
5. No batch-publishing near-identical videos; follow the brief's cadence.
6. A music/SFX licence manifest per episode.
7. Re-check the policy page at each world brief (`platform-research`).
