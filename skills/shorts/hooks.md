# shorts: hooks and structure

## Format facts (2025-2026)
- FACT: Shorts up to **3 minutes** (since Oct 2024), vertical, max 1080p. 60 s is a pacing choice, not a limit. 16:9 under 3 min does not qualify (3P: squares do).
- FACT: since 2025-03-31 a Short "view" = any start or replay, no minimum watch. YPP revenue uses "Engaged views". Public view counts are inflated by swipe-throughs: do not judge hooks by them.
- 3P: the measurable hook score is **viewed vs swiped away** (Studio > Analytics > Content > Shorts, "How many chose to view"). Blogs: 70 %+ good, 75-80 % strong; no official threshold. Compare Short vs Short on your channel, after 48-72 h.
- 3P: average view percentage 70-85 % is "strong" for < 60 s; replays count, so it can exceed 100 % with a seamless loop.
- First 1-3 s must do one thing: stop the swipe. A face or character, motion in frame 1, a line spoken or printed by 0.5 s, a question the viewer wants answered.

## Hook patterns for comedy / story sketches
| pattern | fails when |
|---|---|
| Punchline in reverse: open on the aftermath or the worst line, rewind ("40 s earlier") | the aftermath is less funny than the build-up; you spoil instead of tease; the payoff must be a different joke than the opener |
| Open loop / question | never closed or closed weakly ("and that's all?") |
| Visually odd first frame (a wrong prop at a desk) | the odd image has nothing to do with the joke |
| Text-on-screen hook, 3-6 words | reading time > 1.5 s; collides with UI; repeats the voice at the same time |
| In medias res (mid-argument) | the viewer cannot tell who is who in 2 s: colour-code puppets, fixed positions |
| Pattern interrupt (silence then a shout; a hard paper cut) | used every 2 s becomes noise: one per Short |
Strongest asset for a voice comedy: open on the funniest or most absurd *sentence* that works standalone, not on narration. Never a slow context line ("Привет, это новый выпуск...").

## Audio at the start
Audio at t=0, no pre-roll silence (trim the first 100-200 ms), sound slightly before picture, one distinct SFX is fine (a paper rip), no music bed under the first line, start mid-breath, no greeting, the speaker's name never first.

## Beat sheet (<60 s, target 40-50 s)
```
0.0-1.5   HOOK      strongest line / odd image / question. no context
1.5-6     SETUP     who, where, what is wrong. one fast exchange (1 premise, 2 characters max)
6-15      ESCALATE  2-3 rapid beats, each worse; re-hook at ~8-15 s (new info, new voice, visual change)
15-35     TURN      the complication; the "rule" gets broken
35-45     TWIST     the hook's answer, or the hook re-contextualised
45-50     TAG       a 2-4 s button / second joke that also bridges the loop
```
A new visual or audio beat every 2-3 s (a static frame > 3 s is a defect); twist in the last 20 % but not in the last second (leave room for the laugh); loop ending: last line or frame matches the opening frame or ends mid-sentence the first line completes.

## The 15-line hook checklist
1. First frame is a character in motion, not a title card, logo or empty paper.
2. Audio starts at t=0; voice line by 0.5 s; leading silence trimmed.
3. Hook line = funniest standalone sentence or an open question; no greeting.
4. Readable in <= 1.5 s with sound off (image + <= 6 words).
5. First frame matches what the video delivers (no fake-out).
6. One premise, <= 2 characters, clear by 6 s.
7. New beat every 2-3 s; one re-hook at 8-15 s.
8. Twist in the last 20 %, then a 2-4 s tag; nothing after the laugh except the loop bridge.
9. Last frame/line links to the first; play twice and check the seam.
10. Burned-in subtitles: 2-4 words, speaker colours, punch word highlighted, 55-70 % height.
11. Nothing important in the top 12 %, bottom 30 %, right 15 %.
12. No spoken CTA; comment bait only as a real question in on-screen text.
13. Branding: constant visuals + numbered title, 1 s corner mark; no intro; sting <= 0.5 s at the end.
14. Title and metadata honest; each episode has an original script, voices and scenes.
15. After 48-72 h read viewed-vs-swiped and the retention curve; adjust the first 3 s first.

## CTA and series
- Explicit "subscribe" lines lower late retention (FOLK); prefer comment bait that is part of the joke, as on-screen text in the tag ("Какое правило вы нарушаете чаще?"); not in every Short.
- Brand in *constant visuals* (paper texture, a recurring puppet, a palette, a fixed corner mark) and in the numbered title ("Правила #7"), never in intro time.

## Fair craft vs manipulation vs penalised
| trick | verdict |
|---|---|
| loop ending; delayed reveal that pays off; re-hook at 8-15 s; progress bar | fair craft |
| comment bait with a real question | fair |
| fake-out first frame; "watch till the end for X" with no X; comment bait by planted error | manipulative (trust loss; may breach deceptive-metadata policy) |
| misleading title/metadata; spam uploads | penalised |
| mass-produced near-identical content | penalised for monetisation (July 2025 "inauthentic content") |

## Outline-first (idea selection, from the outside workflow)
Score 10 situation x place pairs for: clear in 1 second? has a twist? showable as simple pictures? Keep the top 3, write 5 hook lines of <= 12 words for the winner. See `world-bootstrap/idea-matrix.md`. Their 5-piece explainer script is for explainers, not dialogue comedy; keep the *principle* (hook in 5 s, twist near the end).
