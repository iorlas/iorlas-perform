# shorts: cutting a 3-minute sketch to 45-60 s

**Keep:** one premise, 2 characters, the 3-4 strongest exchanges, the final punchline, the most distinctive voice line.
**Cut:** greetings, context, the second joke thread, any exchange whose payoff needs earlier setup, pauses > 0.4 s (tighten to 0.15-0.3 s; speed up at most 5-10 %, check the voice stays natural).
**Restructure:**
1. Pull the funniest *line* to the front as the hook, even out of order; a hard paper-cut transition marks the rewind. Re-record or re-render the opening so the Short is not a lower-quality excerpt of the long video.
2. Compress setup to 1-2 lines. If a joke needs 40 s of setup it is long-form: publish it as 16:9, or as a Short up to 3 minutes (legal); A/B a 55 s cut against the full sketch (viewed-vs-swiped, average view %).
3. End on the reaction or punchline, then the loop bridge.
4. Re-derive the design: portrait shots need one subject each (see `spec.md`): the cast of a wide two-person shot becomes alternating singles.
5. Re-run the checklist (hooks.md) and the table read on the cut: a cut can create holes (a reaction removed, a reference left dangling).
Process: the cut is a **new scenario file** (a short-scene route in `scenario` 7c), not a trim of the wav; render fresh so pauses and rhythm are real.
